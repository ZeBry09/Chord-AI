## Chord detection models

The original source archive's `alpha-model-provenance.txt` states that the Basic/Precise sequence model and live-recording stream model were extracted from a third-party application by Machinita Technologies. It explicitly states that **no open-source or redistribution license for those model weights was established**.

Accordingly, do not assume those model files may be published, redistributed, or relicensed. An open-source license for a model runtime does not license the model weights themselves.
